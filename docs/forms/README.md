# Expert judgment forms and consent statements

`DADE_expert_forms_and_consent_AppendixF.pdf` is Appendix F of the MSc thesis, as built on 1 November 2026.
It gives the full text of every online form used to collect the expert judgments (the AHP form, the three
review forms, the repeat form, the personal scenario forms of the six experts and the form of the four
external practitioners), each in the Arabic original copied without change from the form service on
25 October 2026 and in the researcher's English translation, including the consent question that opened
every scenario form. The forms are reproduced as text; no response, name or employer is included, except
that the three review forms quoted each expert's own earlier answer, which is already public in coded form
in `data/ahp_expert_judgments_anonymized.json`.
